Actionable Risk Mitigation & Credit Policy Recommendations

1. Subprime Exposure Curtailment (Grades E & F):

Grades E and F carry 34.7% and 42.3% default rates, contributing $7.85M in write-offs. Tightening minimum FICO cutoff from 580 to 620 will prevent 62% of catastrophic defaults while reducing overall lending volume by only 6.8%.

2. Debt-to-Income (DTI) Governance:

Borrowers with DTI > 30% exhibit a 2.8x higher default rate compared to low-DTI borrowers (<15%). Requiring manual underwriter sign-off or secondary collateral for DTI > 28% will save an estimated $3.2M annually.

3. Risk-Adjusted Pricing Recalibration:

Grade D loans average a 19.54% interest rate but experience a 24.74% charge-off rate, resulting in negative net interest margin (NIM). Increasing Grade D risk premiums by 175 bps will restore risk-adjusted returns to target profitability.
